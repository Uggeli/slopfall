# Subsistence — food costs ingredients, and the destitute steal

A V4 (economy) slice that pulls **S4-crime** and sets up **L2-migration**. It removes the last
free lunch in the sim and makes coin matter the way it did in a feudal town: you eat what you
**grow, buy, or steal** — nothing is conjured.

## The finding that pulled this (the soak, 40 days, regional Betony — *passes*)

The regional soak passes its **structural** gate (money supply stabilises once settlements export
to balance imports), yet underneath it: **78% broke, Gini 0.95, richest/mean 357×, 296 needs
pegged near max, provisions piling unsold on shelves (840→1197).** The pass/fail gate only checks
*money-supply stability*, not *distribution or welfare* — so "soak green" was hiding a town where
four-fifths of people are destitute and begging. That destitution is what distorts every cognitive
layer downstream (everyone begs → alms mostly refused → social cooling → S2/S3/S4 trained on a
population in freefall).

**Root cause — not unemployment, not money supply.** Everyone *is* employed (`SeedFarmAndEmployment`
puts every resident on a farm/fishery; keepers run shops), and survival is *already free*
(`EatHome` is `JustSustain`, zero coin). The universal begging is generated by one line:

```
DriveCatalog.cs   CoinDef = Derived            (a need axis)
NeedsSystem.cs    deficit = 1.0 - coin         (poverty = "I have no coin")
```

**Being cashless is defined as a maxed need.** A fed, housed, employed peasant with an empty purse
sits at `pov ≈ 0.98` and begs in his spare cycles — regardless of whether he needs to buy anything.
The free-food valve (`EatHome`) is *why* this is stable: nobody has to buy anything to live, so
there's no real food demand to fund wages, so wages stay sub-subsistence, so purses empty, so
everyone begs. A frozen demand-collapse loop the pass/fail gate doesn't see.

## The fix (design, locked with the user)

Coin is a **means, not a terminal need.** Make food cost real ingredients, and the want-for-coin
falls out of the **prepotent hunger drive** instead of a synthetic `1 − coin`.

- **Food is no longer free.** `EatHome` consumes **provisions from a household larder** (reuse the
  existing `Good.Provisions` — no new good type; the larder is a per-home provisions store,
  parallel to a shop's `StockRegistry`). Services and goods already cost coin — now nothing is free.
- **Three ways to get provisions into the larder:**
  - **Buy** — coin → provisions at a store (the `Buy` activity, which *already* says "refill
    household provisions"; now it physically does).
  - **In-kind from the job** — a farm/fishery hand takes home a share of the harvest (provisions →
    their larder) **plus a small cash wage**. The manorial channel: most subsistence never touches
    coin, which is what stops the whole population having to clear the coin bottleneck.
  - **Steal** — `Take` provisions without paying (the destitute tier's survival path), charged as
    crime (S4).
- **Demote `CoinDef`.** It stops being `1 − coin`. Hunger (prepotent, the loudest drive) now drives
  coin-seeking honestly — *I'm hungry → food needs provisions → provisions need coin → I work/beg/
  steal for coin.* Coin-want survives only as a weak discretionary pull (drink, wares). The exact
  shape is settled against the famine soak (see sub-step 3), per "tune after, not during".

### Three classes fall out, for free

| Class | Eats by | Coin for |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary producers** (farm/fishery hands) | in-kind from the harvest | a little — drink, wares, tax |
| **Other employed** (keepers, guards, craftsmen) | buying provisions with wages | food + the rest |
| **The cut off** (no job / no coin / hungry) | begging, **stealing**, or starving | — |

Begging collapses from *universal* to the **genuinely squeezed** — which is what finally makes the
already-seeded S4 begging-shame / stigma measurable, and turns theft into a hunger-driven moral act
(crime-as-tag with real stakes) rather than a stubbed verb.

## Build order — sequence the hunger cliff

Hunger is **prepotent** and free `EatHome` was the only thing holding it. Flip it off before the
ingredient supply chain reliably delivers and the next soak is instant mass starvation. So build
the supply ecology first on a still-fed population, then flip once with every survival path live.

1. **Plumbing (behavior-preserving).** `LarderRegistry` (per-home provisions), wired into
   `SimulationContext`. `EconomySystem` (its sole writer) fills it: `Buy` deposits bought
   provisions → buyer's larder; farm/fishery work deposits in-kind provisions → worker's larder +
   the small cash wage. **`EatHome` stays free; `CoinDef` unchanged.** Soak gate: larders fill,
   provisions flow, nothing regresses (no famine yet — just laying pipe).
2. **Steal-to-eat (dormant).** Activate the reserved `Take` verb (`ActionCatalog`). A `Steal`
   activity takes provisions from a shop's stock → thief's larder, no coin; charge
   `(self, Take, not-mine)` with S4 conscience valence (`ConscienceRegistry`). Free food still
   present, so it rarely fires — but the path is built and tested in isolation. (Witness →
   punishment *installer* stays deferred; the **tag + guilt** land now.)
3. **Flip the valve + rewire CoinDef.** `EatHome` now consumes larder provisions and is **gated**
   on them (mirror `NeedsSystem.SellerHasStock` → a larder gate); remove the free path. Demote
   `CoinDef`; candidate shape: derive `GoodsDef` from the actual larder level (parallel to
   `CoinDef`-from-coin today) and let coin-want bite only when you can't afford to refill a low
   larder. **Expect a famine soak — that is the signal, not a failure.** Add a "**can they eat?**"
   soak metric (hunger-met / starvation count) to replace the meaningless `broke%`. Tune wages /
   yields / prices **once**, after.

The famine is the feature: a starving town is the **"famine drives migration"** driving behavior —
it pulls L2 migration (people leave), S4 crime (steal bread to eat), and life-or-death charity
(warm/cold stigma-vs-pity with real stakes). One slice, four systems.

## Data-shape decisions (locked)

- **Larder is per-home / household** (keyed by `Residency.BuildingIndex`, shared by a home's
  residents). Reuses `Good.Provisions`; single writer is `EconomySystem` (it already owns `Stock`).
- **Farm hands get in-kind food + a small cash wage** (not pure manorial) — a sliver of coin keeps
  the bottom class's aspiration (the occasional drink/ware) and gives the eventual crime layer a
  pull.
- **Steal targets shop stock first** (shoplifting); burglary of other larders is later.

## Discipline (carried)

- **Proto behavior-pull.** This slice exists because the soak demanded it and a chosen behavior
  ("famine → migration / theft / charity") pulls a coherent cross-system slice. Don't over-build.
- **Tune after, not during.** All new constants (`InKind…PerMinute`, the demoted-`CoinDef` shape,
  wage rates) are frozen placeholders; one tuning pass on the **regional** soak after sub-step 3.
- **Single-writer + determinism.** `EconomySystem` is the larder's sole writer, inside its existing
  EntityId-ordered walk; `NeedsSystem` only reads it (mirroring the `Stock` gate).
- **Regional soak is the baseline.** A single town is an economy in a void (exports 0 → it must
  deflate); measurement is against `--soakregion Betony`.
